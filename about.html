OUR STORY & PHILOSOPHY

Where Culinary Passion Meets Design

Founded in the historic leafy enclave of Alipore, The Kitchenette was conceived as a vibrant sanctuary where food lovers gather to learn, cook, and celebrate.

THE ALIPORE SANCTUARY

A Kitchen Built for Discovery, Not Just Cooking

Tucked away on Raja Santosh Road, The Kitchenette bridges the warmth of a home kitchen with the precision of a professional culinary laboratory. Designed with imported Carrara marble countertops, professional induction stations, copper accents, and natural daylight, every corner inspires creativity.

Whether you are kneading fresh semolina pasta with Italian olive oils, mastering the delicate folds of har gow dim sum, or savoring an intimate candlelit dinner with closest companions, our studio provides an immersive culinary escape.

OUR VALUES

The Three Tenets of The Kitchenette

Crafted with respect for authentic gastronomy, premium artisanal ingredients, and meaningful connection.

01

Tactile Mastery

No pre-recorded videos or distant demos. Every guest receives their own dedicated workspace, tools, and raw ingredients to feel textures, test seasoning, and master techniques.

02

Artisanal Sourcing

From cold-pressed Tuscan oils and San Marzano tomatoes to French Normandy butter and organic Himalayan herbs, we work exclusively with uncompromised ingredients.

03

Shared Dining Culture

Food is meant to bring people together. At the conclusion of every class, all guests sit together around our communal oak table to share the dishes they proudly created.
